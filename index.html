The Mission

Bridging the gap between AI theory and production deployment.

"Democratize production-ready AI engineering skills."

Most AI education stops at chatbot demos and prompt engineering tricks. The AI Architect teaches what actually gets deployed: agent architectures with memory and tool-use, local LLM pipelines for privacy-sensitive environments, multi-agent orchestration for complex workflows, and automation systems that don't break when the API changes.

Built by Sierra Napier — MPA/MPH, former Senior Data Analyst (City of Fort Lauderdale, DC Government, Accenture), and self-directed AI engineering specialist with 4+ years building agentic systems.
